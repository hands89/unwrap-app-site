Who's behind it

One person, around the shifts.

I'm a serving firefighter and a dad of two boys, and I build Unwrap at home in the North West, around shifts and school runs.

Unwrap isn't here to tell you what to eat. It shows what's on the label, explains it in plain English, and points to simpler options if you want them. What you do with that is up to you.

There are no ads and no money from food companies, and I don't sell anyone's data. It's paid for by the people who choose to support it.
