The Problem

Brigades across the UK have instructed crews to stop storing gloves in helmets and boots. Carcinogen contamination is the reason. No replacement has been issued. Firefighters are carrying contaminated kit loose — in pockets, in bags that were never designed for the job.

Chemical exposure is the second fire. It accumulates silently over a career. Bulwark exists to address it — starting with carry goods that treat decontamination as the foundation, not an afterthought.
