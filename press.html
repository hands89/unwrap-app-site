The story

I'm a firefighter and a dad, and I built Unwrap on my own, around my shifts.

You scan a barcode and Unwrap lists every ingredient in plain English: what each one is, why it's there, and how processed the food is overall. If you want something simpler, it suggests a similar product, another idea, or a make-your-own recipe. The make-your-own cards and recipes are on this website too.

It isn't funded by investors or food companies, it has no adverts, and it doesn't sell anyone's data. People who scan without an account aren't tracked.

Food education, not food fear

Unwrap is about understanding food, not being told off. There's no guilt, no scare stories and no "good" or "bad" foods. It explains what's on the label so people can make their own choices.

It sits alongside cooking and nutrition lessons. The skills are the ones schools are being asked to teach: knowing where food comes from, how it's made, and how to judge the claims on a packet.

What Unwrap is

  • A guide to what's in a product, ingredient by ingredient
  • A processing score from 1 to 10, with the working shown
  • Simpler swaps, make-your-own cards and recipes

What it isn't

  • A health or diet score
  • Medical or nutrition advice
  • A list of foods to ban

The score is worked out only from what is printed on the label. How it works, and where the evidence is still debated, is on the Evidence & approach page.

Schools, groups and food educators

I've written free label-literacy and food education packs that work without the app, and they're ready to use now:

  • Read the Label for primary (KS2): a full lesson or five 10–15 minute sessions
  • Behind the Label for secondary (KS3): a full lesson or six short sessions
  • Food Detectives for Cubs, Scouts, Brownies and Guides: two games-based meeting plans

Every pack comes with teacher or leader notes and a sheet showing the source for every claim. They're free with an adult account on the Education page. If you teach food, run a group or support schools, I'd like to hear what would help you.
