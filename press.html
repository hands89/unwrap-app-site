The story

Eddy is a firefighter and a dad living in Macclesfield. He built Unwrap on his own, around his shifts.

You scan a barcode and Unwrap lists every ingredient in plain English. It tells you what each one is, why it's there, and how processed the food is overall. If you want something simpler, it suggests similar products, other ideas, and easy recipes to make it yourself.

Unwrap is built by one person. It isn't funded by investors or food companies, it has no adverts, and it doesn't sell anyone's data. People who scan without an account aren't tracked.

Food education, not food fear

Unwrap is about understanding food, not about being told off. There's no guilt, no scare stories and no "good" or "bad" foods. It explains what is on the label so people can make their own choices.

It goes alongside cooking and nutrition lessons. The skills are the same ones schools are being asked to teach: knowing where food comes from, how it is made, and how to judge the claims on a packet.

What Unwrap is

  • A guide to what's in a product, ingredient by ingredient
  • A processing score from 1 to 10, with the working shown
  • Simpler swaps and make-your-own ideas

What it isn't

  • A health or diet score
  • Medical or nutrition advice
  • A list of foods to ban

The score is worked out only from what is printed on the label. How it works, and where the evidence is still debated, is set out on the Evidence & approach page.
