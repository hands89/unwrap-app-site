What the score is, and what it isn't

The score runs from 1.0 to 10.0 and measures how processed a food is, based on the ingredients printed on its label. It is not a health score. It doesn't tell you whether a food is right for you.

  • Unwrap gives general information. It isn't medical, nutritional or dietary advice. If you have a health condition, talk to a doctor or dietitian.
  • Allergies: product data can be out of date or wrong, and recipes change. Always read the label on the pack in front of you. Never rely on Unwrap alone to avoid an allergen.
  • Product information comes from manufacturers' labels, from Open Food Facts and from people who use Unwrap. I check what I can, but I can't promise every entry is complete or correct. If you spot a mistake, report it in the app and I'll fix it.

How the score works, and why, is explained on the Evidence & approach page.

Supporter subscriptions

  • Supporter is a monthly subscription on a sliding scale, from £0.99 to £4.99 a month. Every price unlocks exactly the same features. You choose what works for you.
  • You buy and pay through the App Store or Google Play. Their terms apply to the payment. I never see your payment details.
  • It renews each month until you cancel. To cancel on iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions. You keep Supporter features until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own process. If something's gone wrong, email me as well and I'll help however I can.
  • If a price changes, the store will tell you before it applies, and you can cancel before then.

Tips

Tips are one-off payments that say thank you. They don't unlock any features. They're paid through the App Store or Google Play, and any refund goes through their process.
