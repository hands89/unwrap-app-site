The Unwrap app

See it. Scan it. Swap it.

Scan a barcode and see every ingredient in plain English, with the ones linked to heavy processing flagged. Then find a simpler swap, if you want one.

How it works

Three taps.

1

Scan

Point your camera at a food barcode, or snap a supermarket receipt to look over a whole shop.

2

Understand

See every ingredient laid out. Flagged ones are highlighted, each with a plain-English explanation.

3

Decide

Look at swaps, alternatives and make-your-own ideas. Or do nothing. It's your call.

Beyond the score

Swaps, alternatives and Make Your Own.

Under every scan there are three different next steps. Use one, all of them, or none. They're there when you want options, not to tell you what to buy.

Swaps

Same thing, fewer flags

Other products from the same category, like another mayo for your mayo, starting with the closest match.

  • Best score: the lowest UPF score
  • Best value: the lowest price point
  • Simplest: the shortest ingredient list
  • Already holding the best one? Unwrap tells you
Free account · full list: Supporters

Alternatives

Something different that does the same job

Options from a different category that could fill the same gap, with fewer flagged ingredients than what you scanned.

  • Simplest: the shortest ingredient list
  • Fewest flags: a second pick with as few flagged ingredients as possible
  • Whole food idea: a grab-and-go suggestion, no cooking needed
Free account · full list: Supporters

Make Your Own

The simple version, made at home

For products that are easy to make yourself, a short card with the few ingredients you need and the steps.

  • Short and cheap: time, cost and difficulty shown up front
  • No fancy kit, no hard-to-find ingredients
  • Plus full recipes in the Recipes tab
