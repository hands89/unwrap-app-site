How the packs are made

Plain English, no scare stories.

Skills, not rules

The lessons teach how to find and read an ingredients list, what additives are for, and how to check a claim on the front of a pack against the back. Nobody is told what to eat.

Every fact checked

Each pack comes with an evidence and sources sheet. Every claim in the slides, notes and printables is listed with its source and the date it was checked.

Safe for every class

No talk of weight, bodies or calories, and no "good" or "bad" foods. The teacher notes cover sensitive topics, allergies and how to adapt the lesson.
